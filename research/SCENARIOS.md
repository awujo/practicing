# Expanded historical scenario study

Manual workflow: **SLK expanded historical scenarios**, branch `claude-kevin-king`.
Empty pairs means all 28 frozen FX pairs. No broker acquisition, live schema/settings or trading changes; no OpenAI calls.

The study reuses the seven successful frozen IC Markets exports from 19 October 2026. Those Actions input artifacts expire after 30 days; this workflow does not establish permanent raw data storage. About 1,200 days of candles yield about **835 evaluation days** after the first 365 days supply context. The native engine is replayed through successive windows of at most 90 days, using **Daily and Weekly story origins**, completed H4 observation boundaries, code defaults and the actual available prefix. All inspected historical windows are exploratory, with no untouched performance claim.

## Implemented studies

| Family | Explicit alternatives / measurements |
|---|---|
| Post-stop path | Following 24 and 72 real hours: original entry recovered, original target reached, continuing against trade, near actual stopped region or mixed; adverse distance and endpoint relative to original risk. Excludes the stop candle itself; censored gaps/endpoints remain censored. |
| Re-entry | After a stop becomes observable, first new closed-H1 break of the preceding three-bar range, tested as a limit retest at the observed close, with its own structural stop and 2R target. One same-direction re-entry while original story remains valid, and one opposite-direction local setup independently. Opposite local setup is **not a newly confirmed opposite Daily/Weekly bias**. No recursive repeated stop/re-entry loop. |
| Story lifecycle | Keep; retire at target; retire on a newer same-direction or opposite-direction native tap; latest-tap only; require a newer still-valid origin-confirmed same-side story to renew after target; age limits 5/10/20/40 days. New Daily/Weekly rejections are those generated by the existing engine, not an invented new pattern grammar. |
| New versus old stories | Native tap/confirmation records retain contemporaneously known other story counts and direction. Policies compare keeping old signals versus retiring/suppressing them when new native taps/confirmed stories exist. Only observed events within the window are reconstructed; pre-window standing state is not fully recreated. |
| Confirmation | Native lower-timeframe external breaks: H4 for Daily, Daily for Weekly, versus subsequent actual origin candle close beyond point B; origin invalidation, no confirmation or censored outcome recorded. Directional paths are recorded at each observable event; this does not fabricate a tradable early entry or treat a forming candle as confirmed. Waiting-confirmation outcomes are conditional on confirmation occurring and are not directly comparable to all early breaks without accounting for that selection. |
| Entry timeframes | Existing pattern finder on prefixes: H4/H2/H1 zone stops plus H1 rejection stop. The historical study raises the per-window chronological confirmation cap from 12 to 128 and reports any cap reached. |
| Stops | Original; 1.5 times original risk (a risk-sensitivity diagnostic, not necessarily a structural level); extrema of last five completed H1/H2/H4 bars plus two-pip buffer. Levels fixed at entry-decision time. Compare R along with risk-distance ratio; narrower/wider stops do not imply equal units or equal currency risk. |
| Targets | Original entry target; 1R/2R/3R; nearest causally confirmed three-bar H4 pivot among last 60 bars; most extreme level of last 20 completed origin bars. These are specified target proxies, not proof of optimal external liquidity. |
| Exit management | Half at 1R then breakeven, retaining original final target; trailing stop from last three closed H1 candles after fill. Stop modifications become active on the next candle. Same-bar uncertainty remains conservative and flagged. |
| Costs | Baseline one-pip spread and 0.2-pip slippage, plus matched execution sensitivity at 0.5/2-pip spread and 0.5-pip slippage. Commission remains zero, explicitly. Cost alternatives reuse the baseline setup rather than regenerating spread-dependent setups. |
| Context | Daily/Weekly origin, origin/higher trend agreement or unknown, WAT six-hour buckets, weekday feature, volatility relative to preceding 480 H1 ranges, story age, distance from level, rule and level type. Fixed groups, no fitted optimum or causal claims. |
| Paths after tap/target/continuation/end | 24/72-hour favourable/adverse excursion and endpoint; favourable excursion then ending against, adverse excursion then recovery. Measured from the first observed event, not an assumed exact intrabar target-hit time. |

Alternatives vary one factor at a time around the same observable setup. They are **not all Cartesian combinations**. A different structural target may not exist; unsupported candidates are omitted, not replaced by a hindsight level. No lower-timeframe data is synthesized from H1.

## Execution and data limits

H1 bid candles, fixed spread/slippage assumptions, no commission, pending orders expire after 24 real hours and filled positions exit after 24 hours if neither stop nor target occurs. Same-fill-bar profit is not awarded. When stop and target occur in the same hourly candle, stop gets priority and ambiguity is flagged. **M5 or finer execution remains unavailable** until genuine data is obtained; this study cannot resolve those cases or identify economic causes from price alone.

Gap-adjacent H4 cutoffs are excluded for eight days after a missing expected H1 market hour, and event bootstrap/memo are reset. Excluded counts are explicit. Older warmup gaps can still influence context; expected-hour checks do not model every holiday. Success means processing finished, not that every possible historical timestamp had complete data.

Windows reset engine observation state; events already present at bootstrap are context, not manufactured new confirmations. Some entry searches and native outcome windows at boundaries are censored. Scenario path labels can use later frozen candles only as outcomes; candidate selection uses prefixes. Weekly and Daily latest-story contexts remain separate.

## Budgets, artifacts and publication

- Two offline matrix workers; at most ten windows per pair. Each native window gets at most 600 seconds, scenarios at most 240 seconds, and a pair at most 9,600 seconds. These are hard ceilings, not a runtime estimate. A real 90-day EURUSD replay completed in about 41 seconds locally before scenario work.
- Native ledgers capped at 8 MiB, scenario ledgers at 5 MiB, scenario records at 12,000 per window. Interrupted or capped windows are partial, not silently completed.
- A compressed archive is limited to 8 MiB per pair (224 MiB across 28 pairs) and retained for seven days. A larger archive is not uploaded and is reported missing. Download archives before expiry for permanent preservation. Summary retained 30 days.
- MySQL writes remain exclusively `research_records`, with a **128 MiB aggregate research payload budget**, including existing research payloads; indexes/row overhead and other database tables are outside that count. Historical publication stops at the budget, reports errors, and leaves archive results available. The regular-study and history publication jobs share one concurrency group so those workflow writers cannot race the size check; this is not a quota enforced on arbitrary external database writers. This is not a check of the user's account billing quota or remaining DB capacity.
- Aggregate report checks every expected pair, partial windows, missing reports and publication errors. It groups entry alternatives, post-stop classes, re-entry statuses, context and lifecycle policies without choosing a winner. Correlated pairs, overlapping outcomes and repeated counterfactuals are not independent samples.
- This larger run consumes GitHub Actions time/storage. No resident AI checker is needed. No recurring schedule or automatic live-rule promotion.

An independent code reviewer checked chronology, partial sizing, managed-stop references, renewal eligibility, resume identity and missing-pair accounting; regressions cover those cases. Comprehensive research means explicit scenario coverage and honest limits, not a guarantee of a globally optimal system.

## Resumable finalization (20 October 2026)

The original full-history run `39296129468` completed all 28 pair studies and saved all archives. Its monolithic database-saving job timed out after its configured 60 minutes; the research itself was not cancelled mid-study.

`research-finalize.yml` now recovers those archives without replaying data. On its first run it validates all expected pairs/window ledgers, produces the combined `history-summary.json`, and uploads that summary **before** database work. Each subsequent job downloads only the relevant pair archive, saves at most four ledgers within a 420-second cooperative budget, and uploads an atomic checkpoint. It explicitly dispatches the next workflow with that checkpoint. A partially written ledger is retried idempotently, with matching existing records skipped.

Jobs have a 15-minute timeout; at most 200 slices are allowed. Two consecutive unexpected errors or the 128 MiB research payload cap stop automatic continuation visibly rather than looping indefinitely. Cancellation suppresses automatic continuation. Checkpoints and recovered summary are retained 30 days; source pair archives retain their original seven-day expiry. A manual recovery can provide the last `state_run` if dispatch/setup fails; there is no resident AI watcher. Future full-history studies automatically dispatch this short-job finalization after their matrix succeeds.
