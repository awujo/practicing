## NEW (minor): "Elbow structure" caution
When a daily single-candle-liquidity level, viewed down on the 4H
timeframe, doesn't resolve into one clean swing point but instead looks
like a bent, multi-candle "elbow" shape, Bishop treats this as a signal
to WAIT for that broader elbow structure to be fully swept before
trading, rather than acting on the first touch: "most times once I see
that my daily... single candle is an elbow structure on the four-hour
timeframe, I just wait for that to be taken out." A minor, visually-
defined exception case layered on top of the general single-candle/
structural-liquidity concept (Section 3) — not crisply quantifiable from
this description alone, but worth flagging as a caution: when a
higher-timeframe liquidity point decomposes into a non-trivial shape at
the confirmation timeframe, treat the whole shape as the liquidity pool
to be cleared, not just the first candle touched.

## Reinforced: discretionary/experience-based judgment (second data point)
For a pair trading at all-time highs with "nothing clean" on the daily
timeframe, Bishop explicitly traded a sell based on repeated observation
that price kept "disrespecting bullish structure" at that level, admitting
directly: "it's based off of experience... I don't know how to explain
that to make sense actually." A second explicit acknowledgment (after the
"no clear intel" admission in the Daily Routine video) that some real
trades rely on accumulated pattern-recognition experience beyond the
fully mechanized ruleset — worth keeping as an open caveat rather than
something the backtester can fully replicate from the documented rules
alone.
