# Video 15: Entry Models 2

## Content type
Short, single live Gold (XAUUSD) trade walkthrough in real time across
4H -> 1H/30min -> 15min -> 5min, used to directly demonstrate picking ONE
of the entry patterns from Video 14 and sticking to it. Mostly
reinforcement of already-documented mechanics rather than new rules, plus

## "Pick one system and stay with it" (explicit discipline principle, new)
Directly addresses the fact that his own SLK framework offers multiple
valid entry variants (the 3 confirmation-entry patterns from Video 14).
Explicit personal advice, generalized from a 1-on-1 mentoring call: don't
try to trade all variants situationally — commit to ONE preferred pattern
(e.g. always "play the engulfing directly" vs. always "wait for the
open-close break") and apply it consistently, rather than switching
approach setup-to-setup. Framed as a trading-psychology/consistency rule,
not a mechanical rule — does not change what the valid patterns are, just
recommends picking one as a personal default. Worth surfacing as a
configurable "entry style" parameter in the bot rather than a fixed
algorithm choice.

## 4H timeframe emphasis (reconfirmed, strongly repeated)
Repeats "four hours is very, very important" multiple times — 4H is
explicitly called out as a preferred timeframe for identifying the
key-level rejection + close (R&C) that anchors the whole cascade down to
execution timeframes. Consistent with, not new versus, earlier videos'
general bias/setup-on-higher-timeframe framing (Section 7) — this video
just singles out 4H specifically rather than "higher timeframes" generally.

## Real trade walkthrough (Gold, multi-timeframe, live)
- **4H**: three consecutive up candles. First two form an open-close key
  level; the third candle itself serves as IRL. That IRL/key-level also
  overlaps an imbalance. Price played from the open-close key level and
  closed above it — textbook Pattern 1 (direct mitigation) from Video 14.
- **1H/30min**: price broke above the open-close key level, engulfed, came
  back to play (retest) it, then ran to the upside — textbook Pattern 2
  (engulf + open-close break, return to the open-close) from Video 14.
- **15min**: two consecutive down candles formed an open-close; price
  engulfed and broke above it — same Pattern 2 mechanics repeating one
  timeframe lower, explicitly demonstrating the "same pattern applies
  recursively across nested timeframes" principle already implicit in
  earlier videos but not previously shown this explicitly stacked in one
  single live example.
- **At the live moment of recording**: expects a further open-close
  resting just above current price to cause a brief rejection before
  continuation; separately, notes another open-close fully overlapping an
  imbalance just below current price that could serve as IRL — expects a
  small dip to tap it before continuing upward. Explicitly frames this as
  two candidate scenarios being tracked simultaneously, not a single
  fixed prediction.
- **Alternative given for a trader uncomfortable entering directly at the
  open-close/key-level**: drop one more timeframe (15min -> 5min) and wait
  for an engulfing candle to form, then enter directly off that engulfing,
  targeting the same high. Transcript cuts off mid-sentence ("if an
  engulfing is not being created...") — the fallback-if-no-engulfing-forms
  case is not captured in this transcript; may be covered in the video's
  remaining unheard portion or a later video.
