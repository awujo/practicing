## Top findings
- Kevin draws levels as thin horizontal RAYS starting at the wick tip (A/V shape) or body/close edge (open-close level) of the swing candle and extending right; no zones. Only the buy-zone/consolidation shows as a rectangle (drawn loosely, then deleted).
- Fresh/used test (key-levels-pt4): a WICK entering the level uses it; a full-body close through it keeps it fresh (and an unfresh level becomes fresh again after a full-body close through). He uses a level once, judges "use" count on higher TF (W/D) not on 1h/4h.
- All real charts are CANDLESTICKS. The LINE chart (closes) is switched on briefly (story-line-pt2, D and 4H replay) only to read swing highs/lows and the HH/HL trend, then back to candles to place rays. A bot should compute swings from closes for structure, but place levels on wicks/bodies.
- Story always flows: higher-TF fresh key level -> candle taps with wick and closes back (tap-reject-close) -> one TF lower: find the last opposite swing (V for sells, A for buys) formed BEFORE the first tap (point B, not the swing made by the rejection itself) -> body close beyond it = external BMS/breakout (labelled bms/EXT) -> later breaks = BOS (internal, entries only).
- Rejection can repeat for days with a sideways H4 range; no trade until the body close beyond B. Drawn as a zig-zag range with a flat line at B, then a drop.
- Alternative origin with no key level: sweep of previous day/week high (wick above, close below) then break of the last V before the sweep; sweep candle itself is not the swing; if price already ran too far it no longer counts.
- Labels he really writes: M/W/D (TF of level), "TAP AND REJECT", "CLOSE", "BMS", "BO", "EXT", "BOS", "A"/"B", "H4", "WRW". No HH/HL text; sweep/IRL/ERL are not labelled in these videos.
- Targets are the next opposite D/W/M ray (the other "M"/"W"/"D" line drawn at the bottom of every schematic); in the CADJPY replay TP = the previous D swing high at ~114.65 with invalidation at the origin level ~112.903.
- Calendar order: M levels set once per month, W per week, D per day; the story starts and concludes on the same TF (start W -> end W, using D for confirmation; start D -> confirmation on H4).
- Origin levels in the real example (CADJPY D) are open-close level at ~112.903 where the daily wick dipped in and closed above; breakout confirmation read on 4H replay with a "bms" trend-line label on the breaking candle.
