# Protection scope: source audit and correction principle

2026-10-24. Read-only source audit prompted by overly distant protection candidates in the p8/p15 diagnostics. No detector, fixture, expected price, tolerance or source execution record is changed. The diagnostic description of an “old H2 reaction low” or “old range high” is a hypothesis about the current engine's scope; source pages alone do not certify the exact software cause.

**The supported correction is to identify the protection object belonging to the selected entry episode/model, then protect additional nearby still-relevant liquidity/KLs only when their relationship to that episode is established.** Source evidence supports both local-pattern protection and wider contextual protection. It does not support automatically using the oldest parent-range origin, automatically using the latest tiny candle wick, or choosing whichever stop gives the desired price/R.

## Application to the two diagnostic concerns

**PDFp8:** source long plan0.85315/0.85289/0.85447. It labels H2 high-swept/low-standing V CE and H4 context, but does not number the candle pair or explain stop selection. A blanket minimum over the whole H2 history since the old H4 reaction is unsupported. A blanket pattern-wick stop is also not proved from the annotation. The source-specific candidate is the standing/protective extreme of the actual H2 CE episode, plus any specifically related still-relevant nearby context. Exact pair and order availability must be resolved before saying which candle supplies it. The old reaction low cannot be retained merely because it is old and lower, nor removed merely because it makes risk wider than the printed plan.

**PDFp15:** independently viewed actual USDCAD H1 source drawing1.40471/1.40539/1.40129, labelled `H1 Breakout Confirmation Entry` and H4-with-idm/H4rH4 context. The source stop is near the currently illustrated short formation/reference region. It is not proof that an older high from a broader historical H1 range protects this CE. If the engine selects that high solely through persistent parent range membership, it needs an entry-episode relationship check. The page does not uniquely identify a mechanical alternative wick or show why the stop equals its exact printed price. Broker arrows on the separate image show a shorter realized leg; they do not independently establish original stop rationale. Source geometry and annotation can reject a claim that the tool itself proves a universal FVG edge, but cannot certify a fully fitted replacement rule.

## Implementable correction principle, with engineering choices separated

The source supports recording a **protection set** whose objects belong to the selected model/episode:

1. Preserve actual entry formation/confirmation evidence and the associated local/sweep-origin high/low, with IDs and first availability. Keep the full story-origin/range references separately.
2. For an origin/sweep-break setup, identify its strong sweep/origin extreme as a protection candidate. For later CE/continuation, identify the **new entry formation's** protective extreme instead of automatically inheriting the earliest story range. A shallow OC entry does not automatically replace the same formation's deeper protective extreme with the OC candle alone.
3. Add a higher-TF KL/liquidity candidate only with a recorded causal relationship to the current anticipated reaction/normal sweep. Examples support doing so, but relevance cannot be reduced to “all older unswept levels” or “all objects in the parent range.”
4. Distinguish structural-safe, direct-extra-KL, local-confirmation and aggressive fixed-stop policies. Emit all documented alternatives if source permits them; pick an operating profile before outcomes. Never shrink or expand a stop because later P&L or the source price suggests a convenient match.
5. Buffer the selected protection boundary under an explicit profile. Core quotes support0.5/1pip examples, not a universal cross-asset number. No reviewed source defines an ATR stop-distance multiplier, an ATR relevance radius, a fixed maximum protection age, or a universal latest-turn rule.

A practical numerical algorithm can use the chosen extreme of the **confirmed entry episode**, outward over its explicitly linked protection set, then declared padding. The definition of episode start/end, strong versus local reference promotion, which nearby H4 objects remain relevant, and conflicts between candidate scopes still require disclosed engineering rules and independent case review. This audit does not pretend the teacher supplied those exact algorithms.

When scope linkage is missing, output `PROTECTION_SCOPE_UNRESOLVED` with candidate IDs/TFs/ages and the missing relationship. Do not silently use a far older origin as a safe default, and do not fall back to a desired-price wick. Unknown protection is an unresolved candidate, not permission to invent an eligible stop. Structural stop invalidation and structural story invalidation remain distinct: a tight entry can lose while the larger story survives.

## Evidence boundary

Source-backed: local and origin-based stops, shallow-entry/deeper-same-formation protection, intentional nearby H4 liquidity/KL protection, different direct versus CE alternatives, and independent numerical aggressive preference. Not established: one universal scope priority or exact formula that guarantees these printed plans. The audit supports correcting **scope attribution and provenance** before changing distance constants; it does not support tuning ATR, buffers or fixture values to force a match.
