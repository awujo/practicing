## Course14: full chronological rules

Source: `videos/video-14-entry-models-1/transcript.txt`.

- 22.7–58.7: range is defined through sweep+break; internal liquidity is created;
  entry cycle takes IRL then seeks ERL. Context precedes the entry pattern.
- 71.7–189.7: two stated market tasks are attacking liquidity and rebalancing old
  imbalance at a specific KL. QMR/A-shaped KL overlapping imbalance is one
  choice. OC overlapping imbalance is another. He explicitly also discusses
  absence of a KL inside imbalance and an OC close to IRL (114.7–148.7).
  Therefore blanket 'every entry requires A/V inside imbalance' overstates this
  lecture. An exact proximity threshold is not stated.
- 189.7–263.7: direct entry may face further liquidity-taking or failure; waiting
  for confirmation is an alternative. After H4 IRL sweep and KL reaction,
  candle closure and lower timeframe inspection matter.
- 263.7–370.7: distinguishes origin from continuation. Continuation follows an
  impulsive break of former ERL/V KL; expect shallow retrace, new IRL near the
  broken KL and imbalance overlap. 345.7–355.7 explicitly says10–11pips,1:3 or
  1:4 **in this continuation discussion**. Claimed70–80% is teacher assertion,
  not independently measured performance or guarantee.
- 370.7–419.7: optional stacking from origin into continuation, potentially two
  more opportunities if conditions form. This is not an instruction to open
  every simultaneous pattern or unlimited portfolio exposure.
- 426.7–496.7: H4 IRL/KL origin → H1 or M30 confirmation. 'One timeframe lower'
  is a conceptual ladder, not necessarily the next numeric chart menu item.
- 496.7–533.7: **confirmation behaviour1:** engulf in anticipated direction;
  line chart reveals newly formed A/V KL; direct mitigation of that KL.
- 533.7–666.7: **behaviour2:** two consecutive candles in the opposite direction
  create OC; direction candle engulfs the final opposite candle AND breaks OC;
  retest broken OC in anticipated direction. For sell stop 'little bit above'
  pattern high (606.7–616.7; transcription 'number four plus' means stop in context).
  Engulf alone is not enough to label this compound OC route.
- 666.7–753.7: **behaviour3:** after engulfing, next H1/M30 candle approaches
  A/V KL without touching; that candle becomes IRL/inducement. Later sweep of
  it then KL mitigation supplies entry. Symmetric buys and sells are explicitly
  acknowledged732.7–744.7. This is distinct sequencing, not arbitrary nextbar fill.
- 753.7–825.7: same confirmation behaviours may apply at continuation. First
  continuation can use10–11pips; after H1 entry moves favourably, optional lower
  M30/M15 confirmation may supply an additional position.
- 1101–1113.5: KL may supply both entries and exits. Exit selection matters.
- 1193–1207: actual gold examples stops below local lows, **not a universal fixed
  11pips**. 1354–1359 another illustration quotes8pip stop. Do not silently
  replace source stops with the user's separate aggressive operating preset.
