## Structural language

The foundational courses distinguish a body close beyond a structural reference
from wick passage with a close back inside. Breaks of one or two structural
points can matter; there is no universal exactly-two rule. A defined advanced
range begins with sweep plus break. Major and nested minor structures coexist.
IRL taken suggests expansion toward ERL; ERL taken suggests retracement/reversal,
but confirmation of a new major story is a separate event.

Dense native-frame review substantially resolves course7's apparent wording
conflict: valid refers to the OLD higher protected high; low-probability/invalid
refers to the NEW lower candidate. A wick-only weak-low sweep keeps the old
protected reference, while the new candidate can supply liquidity. Body-close
break is the explicit contrasting migration case. See independent verification
section1 and the retained native evidence; no numerical pivot algorithm follows.

## Levels are not interchangeable geometries

Course11 repeatedly speaks of closing prices and draws A/V peaks/troughs on a
line. OC is explicitly illustrated using two consecutive same-direction candle
bodies and their open/close junction. The studied definitions do not establish
universal opposite-colour candle pairs or exact O2=C1 equality for all A/V.
Candle gaps, equal-close plateaus and quantitative tolerances remain automation
questions until later source comparison settles them.

A level can be respected and then impulsively disrespected, making a retest in
the new direction relevant. Prior reaction/sponsorship, proximity to liquidity,
imbalance overlap and extreme-versus-QM selection are conditional clues, not
one universally nearest/farthest rule. Neither every level nor every origin is
proved first-touch-only. “No POI” language contrasts broad boxes with specific
prices; later narration explicitly calls the training specific-POI identification.

## Context routes and flips

A KL reaction plus origin candle close and next-lower-TF breakout or sweep+BOS
is a recurring route. A sweep of a previous candle's high/low is an alternative
origin, not an extra mandatory KL condition in every case. M→W, W→D and D→H4
are context ladders; one does not automatically certify the larger timeframe
when a smaller-timeframe story uses its level.

When weekly rejection does not receive Daily confirmation, Daily reactions at
the same weekly level can provide a Daily origin, then H4 confirmation. Plug
specifies repeated Daily reactions; do not impose that exact count globally
without comparing the other examples.

Early lessons allow engulfing in the cascade; later storyline lessons clarify
W rejection→D engulf→H4 structural confirmation. At an existing story's endpoint,
engulf/OC disrespect can offer a retracement entry without confirming a full
flip. A flip needs positive new-direction confirmation, not merely the absence
of the old-direction continuation. The source's own exception concerning an
engulf-started storyline must remain visible.

Same-day and next-day confirmation are both taught. Which origin, breakout or
reaction candle must close is model-specific. Bishop describes personal
confirmation entries before reaction-candle close, while direct H2 explicitly
waits for the H4 breakout candle close. Do not collapse those distinct clocks.
Weekly alignment is preferred in some examples, but Daily/H4 counterweekly
trades are expressly permitted elsewhere. Record dominant direction, retracement
status and nearer-target protection rather than inventing a universal veto.
