## Top findings
- Kevin draws the daily/weekly structure on the LINE chart: a thin horizontal line at EVERY swing low/high (valley/peak of closes) after a break, extended right 15-30 candles (EURAUD, GBPAUD D line charts). Bot: keep a ladder of unbroken swing lows (sell) / highs (buy); a close beyond the latest one = BOS.
- Alignment is checked top-down by sketching the same zigzag expectation on W, D, H4: weekly BOS (close below the last weekly V), then D BOS, then H4 BOS, all same direction. No alignment = "no trade" even if the pair looks tradable (weekly-recap).
- He switches line chart <-> candles constantly: place levels on line chart closes, then judge taps/rejections on candles (wick through line, close back); the lines pass through wicks and sit on bodies.
- Entry on real charts = retest of the last lower-TF A/V shoulder (H4 A-level at ~1.6800 on EURAUD, 1H range break on USDCHF). Zones are small BOXES on the entry TF (candle cluster), not on the key level itself. Contradicts "no boxes" only for entries.
- SL is tiny: just beyond the tapped candle/cluster high (18-19 pips on EURAUD/GBPAUD H4/2H, ~10 pips on USDCHF 1H). TP = point B (the H4 swing low before the tap) or next lower-TF swing; TP1 ~ R 1.4-2, TP2 ~ R 5 (next H4/D level). Never put TP beyond an opposing daily cluster.
- Class-work USDCHF: D range box (rectangle) with price at range bottom; trade only because D key level + H4 key level sit at the SAME price (0.7890) and the D candle rejected it (wick below, close above); then wait for the day to close, next day H4 key level + 1H breakout = entry. With only D reject and no H4 break: no trade.
- Lower TF refinement: same H4 trade shown on 2H, 1H, 30m gives identical SL zone but better entry and R:R (2.1 -> 5.2 on GBPAUD); timeframes used: W, D, H4, 2H, 1H, 30m.
