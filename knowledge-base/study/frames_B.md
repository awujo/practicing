## Top findings
- BOS = body close beyond the swing; sweep = wick beyond a swing with the close back inside. Lines for BOS start at the swing and end at the breaking candle; the sweep line sits at the wick extreme (pt2, pt3).
- Advanced structure order on every chart: sweep (wick) -> BOS (body close) -> inducement (IDM = minor pullback low/high taken out) -> entry from the thin key-level box at origin (pt2, pt3).
- Consolidation: no BOS/BMS inside; use the left side (entry trend) for direction, wait for body-close break, then retest and continue (pt2 gold example with box + trendline).
- Line chart is a teaching/spotting aid only: wicks and IDM disappear; he returns to candles to mark. A bot should detect swings on closes for structure and wicks for sweeps (pt2 t=1060-1100, pt3 t=640-860).
- Sweep vs break test seen on the daily candle drawing: wick above previous high with body below = sweep; body close above = break (pt3 t=1840-2120).
- Zone boxes are thin: from the swept swing's line to the wick/body extreme (pt3 t=560-620; liquidity t=1340 box between IRL line and swept low).
- Trade geometry: entry at IRL (pullback low/zone), stop below the sweep low, target ERL above HH; RR ~4 shown on schematic (liquidity t=1320, pt2 GBPUSD RR box).
- Cycle: IRL -> ERL -> new IRL; after ERL is taken price retraces back to a new IRL (liquidity t=1500-1700, arrows).
- Timeframes seen: 4h and 1h for examples (XAUUSD 2h/1h, USDCAD 4h, EURJPY 4h, GBPUSD 1h, NZDCAD 4h), weekly/daily only for teaching schematics; no numeric params appear.
- SCL: a single candle can supply both ERL (high) and IRL (low), drawn as a box with lines at its high and low (liquidity t=1680-1940).
