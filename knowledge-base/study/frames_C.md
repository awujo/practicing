## Top findings
- Chart type: Kevin reads structure and key-level letters (A/V peaks) on the LINE chart (closes) and uses CANDLES for wicks/open-close; he toggles with the line/candle icon, and TF flow is W -> D/4H -> back. A bot should detect swings on closes and wicks on OHLC.
- Key level (A, V, OC) = two adjacent candles; the line is a thin horizontal at the shared BODY price (A: top of bodies bull->bear; V: bottom bear->bull; OC: two same-direction candles, level at the open/close overlap), wicks ignored; line extended to the right ~10-30 bars; no rectangles/zones, only a grey circle for emphasis.
- Key level flips role: impulsive FULL-BODY close through the level invalidates it; price then retests from the other side (same line) and reacts; drawn as one line used 3 times in the schematics. Invalidation condition = body close (not wick) beyond the line.
- Structure decides which key levels count: in an uptrend sells at A-levels are mostly invalidated (then retest buy) while V buys are valid; opposite in downtrend. Always apply market structure first.
- Wick-to-wick sweep on 4H in same direction as HTF trend (swing low wick swept, close back inside) -> BOS -> inducement low -> return; drawn with a thin box under the swept low; strongest example in key-levels-pt1.
- Single-candle liquidity: lines anchored at candle WICK tips: downtrend candle high=IRL (swept first), low=ERL (target); uptrend mirrored; TF = whatever candle (M/W/D/H4), sequence is sweep candle (wick) then expansion candle taking ERL, may take several candles.
- Trendline liquidity: draw trendline through pullback highs (bull case)/lows (bear case); the HL low line = IRL, the peak high = ERL; sweep of IRL often occurs near end of the staircase; trendline break = pullback over -> targets IRL then ERL.
- Combining order on real charts: structure (HTF zig-zag) -> key level (line at body level of A/V/OC) -> liquidity (sweep of IRL wick into key level, close back) -> target ERL; entry model NOT shown in these videos.
- Fresh vs unfresh key levels: he uses a level once ("fresh" only) to stay mechanical, though levels can be reused; criteria for validity not yet taught.
- Time frames: IRL/ERL examples shown on monthly (XAUUSD), weekly (GBPUSD), daily; key-level examples on 4H/D; all candle charts, sweeps judged by wick vs body close.
