## Top findings
- Sheets opened: introduction 14, introduction-to-entry 23, entry-2 22, entry3 28 (87 total, all rendered).
- introduction is webcam only; nothing to learn visually.
- All entry work is on CANDLE charts (H4/2h/1h), not the line chart; levels are thin horizontal lines at the shared body price of two candles, extended right 5-12 candles.
- Entry model (the "continuation"): two candles form a level, the 3rd candle rejects it with a wick, then a 4th candle breaks out impulsively; enter on the retest of that line.
- After the story is confirmed, only look at H4 internal BOS levels (most recent first); filter to levels with a 3rd-candle rejection; if none, use 2h, then 1h; none = no trade.
- Standard (external) H4 break only confirms bias; entries use the internal breaks that come after price taps the daily KL; skip the very first retracement after the breakout.
- SL sits just beyond the rejection candle (8-21 pips in his examples; use the rejection candle if the zone is large); TP at the story target, R:R shown 3-5; inducement is NOT needed for entries (bias only).
- Confluence: same level showing rejection on both H4 and 2h is stronger; missed entries are normal (price may never retest).
- Entry TF map: monthly story -> weekly entries, weekly story -> daily entries, daily story -> H4 entries (down to 2h/1h as fallback).
- Avoid wide-spread pairs (AUDNZD, NZD/CHF); no other pair preference.
