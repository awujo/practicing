## Cross-source corrections already established

1. **Bias flip needs positive opposite confirmation**, not merely disappearance
   of continuation in the old direction. v16@1617–1707 and v17@171–258,
   391–433 require next origin-TF KL reaction/close plus next-lower-TF breakout
   or sweep+BOS in the new direction. Old v16 notes' absence-only state-machine
   wording is incorrect.
2. **Engulfing has a role, not one universal meaning.** Early v9/v11/v12 permit
   it in the initial cascade. v17@1133–1191,1280–1318 explains W rejection→D
   engulfing→H4 structural confirmation. At the endpoint of an existing story,
   engulfing/OC break alone may give a retracement entry, not a confirmed flip
   (v17@821–1052,1203–1234,1339–1454). Preserve the spoken exception
   “unless it was engulfing that started the previous storyline” @1133–1144;
   it does not license ignoring structural context.
3. **IRL belongs to the appropriate setup stage.** v12@1489–1503 is qualified
   agreement with a student's proposed origin/continuation distinction; it is
   not a proof of an exception-free law. At the HTF origin IRL is shown as
   necessary @1507–1559. After that HTF IRL has already been swept and the HTF
   closes appropriately, the lower-TF engulf entry explicitly need not wait
   for *another* fresh IRL @1704–1828,1899–1915. v11@884–897 already admits
   immediate retest without fresh IRL; v13@475–584 presents alternatives.
4. **QMR/QM is not safely dismissed as generic filler.** v17@1605–1698 contrasts
   a tested/broken QM with an extreme A level and prefers one/twice-held then
   impulsively broken levels. Spoken A/V labels momentarily conflict; the
   actual geometric referent must come from the drawing. Keep this unresolved
   rather than substitute a generic nearest level.
5. **No-POI language does not prohibit planning levels or limit orders.**
   v12@53–108 contrasts broad POI boxes with specific levels; v13@1457–1468
   explicitly calls the training identification of specific POIs. It is not
   evidence that levels may only be identified after a reaction.
6. **External-taken→reversal is a contextual expectation**, not a guaranteed
   structural flip. v18@417–450 explicitly calls it retracement versus IRL
   expansion; v16/17 impose separate positive flip confirmation. Body-close
   line-chart structural references and wick sweep extremes must stay distinct
   (v9@805–814; v18@745–807).
7. **No universal nearest/farthest entry ranking.** Proximity to expected IRL,
   prior respect then impulsive disrespect, structure and imbalance guide
   selection. v11@1232–1287, v13@538–567, v17@1633–1698 and v18@1402–1449,
   1580–1611 describe conditional preferences and uncertainty. Percentages are
   teacher claims with no independently established sample, not measured odds.
8. **No next-day-only rule.** v17@1248–1272 explicitly allows same-day or
   next-day H4 confirmation; completed origin and confirmation clocks still
   cannot be known before the relevant closures.

## Rule interpretation issues to resolve before coding

- Mathematical major/minor structure, displacement/impulse magnitude,
  close-level tolerance and tie logic are not numerically specified by these
  passages. Need visual derivation and explicit choices, not hidden constants.
- Distinguish planning before touch (direct model), confirmed HTF closure,
  initial story and ongoing/reversal structural confirmation. Preserve when
  the information becomes known; hindsight examples alone do not solve it.
- Select expected IRL, then nearby valid KL in structure; not every candle
  high/low is interchangeable, not every KL in the path supplies an entry.
- Role flip of a level is distinct from structural storyline flip. Reused
  levels are explicitly shown; unknown allowed lifetime/retest count remains.
- Structural endpoint, entry-TF ERL first exit, fixed3R management, structural
  stop and aggressive fixed10–11 preference are separate policies. This
  source set does not establish universal fixed stops or compulsorypartials.
- Chart tools and narrated retrospective opportunities do not by themselves
  establish broker execution. Missing price/time/status stays missing.
