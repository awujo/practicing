## The PDF's teaching sequence, without inventing a universal pipeline

Many examples explicitly annotate Daily direction/rejection or swept previous
Daily liquidity, H4 breakout with inducement/H4 rejection, then a lower-timeframe
CE. This is strong evidence for studying that progression. It does not establish
that every page shows all required steps, that every breakout is identical, or
that all examples are completed trades. Several pages are merely paired context
charts; others are one position drawing or a mobile chart; p6 explicitly shows
an unfilled BUY LIMIT at the moment captured.

The model families actually written on charts include engulfing confirmation,
breakout confirmation, breakout with inducement, and an H2 high-swept/low-standing
V-shaped KL attributed to 4thman. The TFs include M30, M45, H1, H2, H3 and H4.
Stacked first/second entries appear on USOIL p10, GBPJPY p19 and GBPAUD p59–60.
Each drawn position has its own risk boundaries; do not combine their stops or
assume repeated views are independent trades.

A TradingView position drawing's pink/grey and teal regions define drawn loss
and profit spans. Their edges therefore coincide with its entry, stop and target
by construction. **That alone cannot prove those regions are independently
identified imbalance/FVG boundaries.** Source bar geometry, the underlying level
and spoken stop rationale must establish that separately. The old assertion
'35 trades, zero exceptions, universal FVG-edge stop' is stronger than the
visible evidence supports.

## Detailed examples and what each proves

### EURCHF p5–6: planned order and target variation

p6 clearly prints BUY LIMIT 0.91, 0.93193, SL0.93147 and TP0.93704. The
current plotted market is above that pending buy limit. This proves a configured
pending order with risk4.6 FX pips; it does not show its actual fill, eventual
outcome or initial issuance clock. The chart-plan target on p5 is recorded as
0.93498 in the existing direct evidence ledger, whereas the broker pending target
is0.93704. Do not merge these into a single universally agreed plan. The p6
numbers imply planned reward/risk approximately11.1; that is an observation,
not a fixed R target for other examples.

### EURGBP p7–8: attribution is not a separate formal algorithm

p8 literally writes 'H2 High swept, Low standing / V-shaped kl / 4thman's CE'.
'CE' is consistently expanded as 'Confirmation Entry' elsewhere in the deck,
but p8 itself contains only the abbreviation. The source also labels the H4
breakout and H4 breakout-with-idm/H4RH4 below it.

Visible candle sequence includes an upward move, declining candles revisiting
the V-body junction around0.85315 while the lower extreme remains below, then
strong upward candles toward0.85447. Not every referenced high/low is separately
numbered, so the precise two candles and sweep comparison that the author chose
cannot be certified solely from the annotation arrow. The printed risk boundary
is0.85289, not the old note's entry0.85319. Snapshot header Jul23 2026 19:08UTC+1
is the image creation clock, not a demonstrated order fill.

It is unjustified to conclude simply from '4thman's' that there exists a wholly
separate fourth-man algorithm absent from the present engine, or that this
alone explains zero engine setups. It may attribute a mentor/community pattern
already represented by the high-swept/low-standing CE family. That mapping
requires explicit comparison with the relevant spoken examples and bar sequence.
Until then: **attributed structural CE, exact implementation mapping unresolved**.

### AUDUSD p24–25: contextual weekly alignment, not universal veto proof

p24 says 'Weekly recent breakout bearish, ERL's standing' and 'Daily recent
breakout was bullish, Looking for shorts to the liquidity, More confidence since
weekly is bearish.' The paired H4 chart labels breakout-with-idm/H4RH4.
This directly supports taking a short retracement against a recently bullish
Daily structure while agreeing with weekly bearish direction. It does not, by
itself, prove that all counterweekly trades are forbidden. The secondary What
Next note explicitly describes a counterweekly exception; the session-study
reader must decide the qualified rule from that primary narration.

### GBPNZD p30–31: execution evidence and management differ from chart plan

p31 is a genuine broker order-history record, not merely an arrow. It prints
buy0.33, open2.30008, close2.30172, displayed SL2.30017, TP2.30672,
commission-2.31 and profit31.52. The chart position drawing prints entry2.29993,
stop2.29842 and target2.30503. The displayed broker stop lies above the buy entry:
it cannot establish the original loss boundary. Do not back-compute original R
using it. The visible broker date/time strings give a route for eventual clock
alignment, but their timezone is not established by the chart snapshot header.
