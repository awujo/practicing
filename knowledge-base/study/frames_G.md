## Top findings
- Chart usage: structure/labels are drawn on candles (thin short lines at body/swing level with text HH, HL, BOS, IDM, BO, EXB); line chart is switched on only briefly to find the last V/sweep/IDM, then back to candles.
- Tap-reject candle: wick through the KL, body closes back on the original side; he waits for the origin TF candle to close (W: Friday close) before moving to the confirm TF.
- Point B = BO = the LAST V (for sells, last V-shaped low on the line chart) formed BEFORE the candle that tapped the KL; he hides the tap candle (replay) to find it; later breaks are only "internal".
- The confirm TF accepts two patterns: (a) sweep+break (QM) or (b) plain break of the last V; W origin -> D confirm, D origin -> H4 confirm, M -> W.
- The H4 breakout is a bias only; the day must also CLOSE beyond the breakout level, otherwise cancel (daily rejection wick above the level on the same day = cancel).
- When a weekly KL is also a daily KL (two TFs overlap) treat it as "daily reject daily" and use the weekly target as TP.
- Entry/SL/TP seen on charts: entry at the most recent breakout-retest level on 1H/H4 (older entry levels dropped when a newer one with the same criteria forms; a B-series entry beats an A-series), SL under the small zone/V low (USDJPY 14.9p, EURGBP ~16-22p, EURCAD wide 38p example), TP = next KL, RR 1.3-3.
- Position-tool examples show RR is whatever the next KL gives (1.33 wide stop, 2.1, 3.0), not a fixed target.
- "BO/IDM" lines extend only a few candles right of the swing; KL lines extend far right.
- Contradiction to STRATEGY.md: daily-confirm wait of 5 candles is not shown; instead the cancel rule is "no daily close beyond BO". SL pip caps (10-15 USD) are not strictly kept (EURGBP 16-22p).
