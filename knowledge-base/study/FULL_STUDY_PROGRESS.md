## Study completion gate

1. Every unique lesson has full transcript coverage and systematic visual
   coverage, including transitions omitted between15-second samples where
   material interpretation depends on them.
2. Every rule and exception has an evidence reference; source statements,
   visual facts and interpretation are distinguishable.
3. Examples have chronological decision narratives before numerical replay
   expectations are promoted. Plans and drawing tools do not prove execution.
4. A separate reader cross-checks the consolidated rulebook, with disagreements
   resolved from the actual source or explicitly left unresolved.
5. The complete specification identifies any numerical automation choices the
   teacher did not define. Only then resume detector implementation.
