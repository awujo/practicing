# Kevin King "Story line" (building bias): codeable rules

Terms: SL = story line. KL = key level. "A-shape" = swing high KL, "V-shape" = swing low KL, "open-close KL" = body-based level. "Fresh" = not yet touched. Ready-made rules are marked [T video@sec] (transcript) or [F video/sheet] (frame). Video names are shortened: sl=story-line, pt2/pt3/pt4, aio=all-in-1, wr=weekly-recap, cw=class-wrok.

## 1. Procedure

Output = direction + target + invalidation. His own words: "the way we trade is direction, target and entry" [T sl@374]. The SL only gives direction. Entry is a separate class (QMR/CRT/continuation models), not covered here.

Which timeframe to start on, by calendar [T sl@468; T pt4@87-168; T aio@78-118]:
- New month: start from Monthly. Mark monthly KLs once. They stay valid all month, and daily trades go toward the monthly target.
- New week: start from Weekly.
- New day: start from Daily.
- He builds bias only from M, W and D KLs, not H4 or H1 [T sl@397]. Higher timeframe = smaller losses.

A SL "starts on TF X and ends on TF X". Start on M and it concludes on M. Start on W, it concludes on W. Start on D, it concludes on D [T sl@920; T pt2@244-263].

Two-step ladder, always one timeframe lower [T sl@1556; T pt2@288-330; T pt3@188-270]:
- Step 1 (origin TF): price must TAP a fresh KL, REJECT, and the candle must CLOSE (tap-reject-close).
  - Pass: the wick is in the KL and the close is back outside, on the rejection side.
  - Closing is the standard. Trading before the close is allowed but "standard is wait" [T sl@1314-1541].
  - Reason to wait: a mid-month monthly rejection can be cancelled by a weekly breakout the other way before month-end [T sl@2079-2195].
- Step 2 (one TF lower: M->W, W->D, D->H4, H4->H1): need ONE of two things:
  - (a) Sweep of liquidity plus a break of structure ("sweep and break"). OR
  - (b) a plain "breakout".
  - Not both. The sweep-and-break is the "advanced market structure" setup [T pt2@330-436].
- When step 2 is met, the origin TF direction is confirmed, e.g. "weekly confirms monthly is now bearish". Only then is the SL complete [T sl@1704-1730].
- Day-to-day this is called "daily reject daily + H4 breakout" [T pt4@566; T cw@222].

Breakout definition, precise [T pt2@505-650; T pt3@594-840; T pt3@1057-1100]:
- For a SELL from a KL on the origin TF: go to the lower TF and find the LAST V-shape (swing low) created BEFORE price first tapped the KL.
- A candle BODY must close below it. A small close is enough [T pt4@1465].
- For a BUY: the last A-shape (swing high) before the tap, and a close above it.
- This is the EXTERNAL breakout (it plays the role of BMS). It confirms direction.
- Breakouts after the tap are INTERNAL (BOS). They are not used to confirm the bias. They are used for entries and continuation [T pt2@2085-2178; T pt3@1144-1260].
- Example of a nearby swing: the "B" low (last low before the daily tap) counts, not the "A" low made by the rejection day itself [T pt3@1690-1790].
- A wick pierce is not a breakout. It must be a body close.

Output, once confirmed [T pt2@1914-1968; T pt4@1498-1520; T pt4@2143]:
- Direction = away from the KL.
- Target = next KL of the same or higher timeframe. In the examples: next daily KL for a D story, next weekly KL for a W story, next monthly fresh level for an M story [T sl@648].
- TP1/TP2 = obvious liquidity / nearest opposite KLs.
- Invalidation = a body close beyond the origin KL / the last swing the breakout was built on (e.g. weekly "closes below the key level with a full body" [T pt4@383-396]).
- Per-trade invalidation = the stop at the invalidation point; he uses 15-20 pips max [T wr@1590-1610].
- Confirmed bias does not mean enter. Entries are a range of possible zones from the origin KL up to the breakout [T pt4@575; T aio@1957-1979].

Choosing the KL to build from [T pt2@897-1000; T pt2@950; T aio@1708-1750]:
- It must be fresh (untouched). Open-close KLs touched by a body are no longer fresh.
- When several KLs were respected on the way, use the most recent fresh one price reacted from.
- Remove or ignore the older ones.
- First check the basic trend (HH/HL or LH/LL). Look for a bias in the trend direction first [T pt2@769-811].
- The KL type can be A-shape, V-shape or open-close.
- Alternative origin with no KL: a sweep of liquidity (previous day/week high or low) followed by a break of the last swing before the sweep. The sweep candle itself must not be the swing [T pt3@1887-2090; T pt4@1349-1480].

## 2. Confirm / flip / cancel / no-trade

- No external breakout means no SL means no trade. Repeated: "No breakout, no story line, no entry" [T pt3@689-726; T pt4@816-826,1161-1187]. Quotes the 30-second rule: if a pair takes effort to read, skip it [T pt4@1195-1217].
- Price rejects the KL for many days with no H4 breakout (a range) means wait. Eventually the breakout can still come on day 3-5 and is valid. A single clean rejection followed by an immediate breakout is the best case [T pt3@360-536, 1337-1420].
- Rejection alone does not give bias. Example: GBPAUD (pt4@1900-2207): weekly rejection up, but price was already reacting from weekly and daily levels and was bearish on W/D/H4. He leans sell, and the weekly bounce is not enough.
- Flip: a daily KL (the one in the path to the target) is tapped, rejected, closes the other way, AND H4 breakout the other way. Then the old daily story is over even if the target was not reached [T pt2@1779-1832].
- Counter-bias with no close beyond the KL: the bias holds [T pt2@1845-1870].
- Monthly bullish and weekly bearish (or the reverse) means no trade. Wait until two higher TFs agree (MTF) [T cw@1236-1312].
- Multi-timeframe alignment for continuation: weekly structure break, then daily same-direction break, then H4 same direction. Missing alignment means no trade [T wr@151-282, 626-670, 2098-2138].
- If weekly gives the structure break and daily has not yet, wait for daily (maybe on Friday) [T wr@2138-2160].
- Overlap rule: two TFs sharing the same KL. Example daily and weekly the same level, daily reject daily, no H4 breakout. Then wait for a new fresh daily KL created by the reaction and use its last swing as the breakout [T aio@1424-1620; T aio@1838-1910].
- Overlap with weaker TF: H4 reject on a level shared with a daily KL but no daily/H4 breakout: only H4 story with H1 breakout. Do not swing it; cap TP at the next H4 level, never above a stronger opposing daily zone [T cw@480-620, 1040-1130].
- Origin must be fresh, per above.
- Consolidation rule of thumb: price usually breaks out in the direction it was going before the range ("not always") [T pt3@955-1051].
- Weekly/daily KLs are rarely disrespected. In a trend, price comes back to the most recent breakout level or V/A-shape KL [T wr@720-780, 1506-1530].

## 4. Numbers, sessions, and vague points to resolve

- Candle closes: daily close = NY close presumably (not stated). Weekly close = Friday, month close = last day of month. Not stated, decide.
- Stop 15-20 pips maximum [T wr@1590-1630]. Entry is not part of the bias.
- "Fresh" = never touched after creation (body for open-close). Not defined for wicks. He treats an open-close KL as spent when a body entered it [T pt2@971-978].
- A-shape/V-shape: not defined mathematically (no fractal length said). Candidate rule: a swing high/low that price left with an impulse, pivot on the chart's timeframe, last one before the tap.
- "Full body" close: body close, no size threshold. "Small close" counts [T pt4@1465].
- Tap-reject-close: wick into the zone, close outside. Not specified if the close must be on the far side of the KL boundary (assume yes). Also for open-close KL a body-in-zone is not a rejection.
- Overlap, multiple KLs, "most recent" selection: prefer the most recent fresh KL that price reacted from. Needs a tie-break rule.
- Higher TF dominance: higher TF KLs are stronger ("monthly is stronger than weekly") [T sl@979].
- Waiting time: no cap. Rangey H4 can last days. No time stop stated, he just waits.
- No explicit session/time-of-day for the bias. Times in captions are his local time (UTC+1). Weekly recap and the "start with D/W/M" rule are the only timing rules.
- Frames partly show the same chart tool (TradingView, replay mode) as the transcript says. No contradiction found. The pt2 transcript calls levels "V-shape/A-shape" with Whisper confusion ("A ship", "eye"). Treat "eye" as "high", "I/Eye" as high, "look" as low. "Ship" = shape.
- Whisper artifacts: "essay" = SL (stop loss), "QMR/QMRO" = his entry model, "CRT" = candle range theory model.
- A bias for a pair that is "no trade" is the default whenever step 1 or step 2 is missing.
