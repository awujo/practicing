## Combined understanding before implementation

These first eight lessons define one interconnected language: structure says
which range and direction matter; liquidity identifies what can be swept and
where price may travel; KL gives a specific reaction price; imbalance is a
separate area potentially overlapping that KL. A/V/OC, wick extremes and full
imbalance zone must not be collapsed into the same object.

Origin, continuation and entry confirmation are separate stages. A teacher can
miss origin yet enter continuation, refine H4 to H3/H2/H1 or M45, or wait for
M15/M5 confirmation. Some direct/aggressive paths omit inducement. A universal
inducement/FVG/first-touch condition would therefore erase taught alternatives.
Likewise every lower structural break cannot automatically reverse the major
storyline. Later bias/storyline lessons must settle the complete hierarchy.

Stops and exits are model/management choices, not one universal fact. These
lessons explicitly show 10-pip personal preference and 3R; they also recount a
10-pip stop-out, a trade reaching3R before a loss toward a6R structural target,
and missed entries. Those observations must survive the final specification.
A chart tool gives a plan; words such as “we took” need execution evidence
before promotion to a strict historical fill case.
